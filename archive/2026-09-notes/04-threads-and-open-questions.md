# The Shortsword: Live Threads, Planted Seeds, Open Questions

## Where each thread stands at the end of Ch 25

**Varen / Mara / Ryn** — the Blighted Murel is dead, Ryn has his sample, and they're due to walk back to Ordenmere for the payment at the western gate (three gold owed to Mara) and the bounty (six Corrupter paws hanging in a tree). They don't know the city is under attack. Their arrival is the obvious collision.

**Sera / Seris** — Sera knows the rot is a dead god, that her magic was never divine, and that Providence may be the last goddess alive. Seris knows there's a quality of mana above anything she can produce, and she's fighting Emmeline over the Wand of Rime.

**The attack** — the Godless Hand's plan has failed on all three fronts. Silas is dead, Cedric's elbow is broken, Vorne is gutted, the northern compound and Haes are gone, Elysia and Severin are fighting three clerics with an artifact, and Seris has Emmeline on the ground. **The western beacon pulsed three times** and Cedric's smile died. The meaning of three pulses is the cliffhanger.

**Caen / the Order** — Caen is managing the Weaver through Lyris, and the name "Yharim" is loose in the world. Varen said *I'm going to find him* to Mara, and Lyris heard it, but not *why*. Caen assumes the Weaver will invent his own answer.

## Planted seeds (things set up that haven't paid off)

1. **The sword.** Caen left a deliberately broken blade with a restored edge and a neglected hilt. It cuts what should be uncuttable (the Blighted Murel), shrugs off acid that melts steel, pulses like the creature's heartbeat in Varen's hand, and pulls him toward the nerve cluster. Ryn examined it and found only "ordinary steel". Where it came from, who broke it, and why is unanswered, and Varen has never asked.
2. **Ryn's five years.** The gold-haired woman, the tree, the spring field. "You're making that face again" became **"What have you done to me."** The fragments fire near Sera (gold hair) and the longest one came with only a wall between him and sleeping Varen. Something took his memory, and the file is getting thicker and the intervals shorter.
3. **Varen's blue anima.** Lyris couldn't find its ceiling, the Weaver got "very interesting news", and the Devourer's system hasn't touched him. Caen's plan for him is still secret.
4. **Caen's plan.** He spent twelve years on a child when adults are efficient, went dark to the Order for years, and his last act at the cabin was to sit in cold ash. He has "nothing left to lose" if the plan fails.
5. **Mara's debts.** Alister still owns her, and she's now carrying gold and has been away for days. She has also chosen someone over profit for the first time.
6. **The commission.** They never registered the job, so the reward is what the board said and not more. The paws are proof.
7. **The philosopher stone**, the Shimmer, the Abyss, Silva's body: Ryn will find it early, without looking.
8. **Seris's route.** The numbered stories in the children's book can reconstruct the traveller's journey to where the dragon story came from, and someone there may still know. Leaving means leaving five students who stopped being a transaction.
9. **Quality of mana.** Sera's viscous, non-dissipating, rising mana may be what the rune-makers used. Whether it is "the blessing of Providence" is deliberately unresolved.
10. **The three-pulse beacon** and whatever the Godless Hand's real target beyond the western district was.
11. **The Weaver's position.** Caen told him the Devourer will eventually see him as sustenance rather than a general, and it landed.
12. **The corruption reaching the walls**, which Maret says will happen in Sera's lifetime.
13. **Varen and Providence.** "Do you think the church would know where she is." Varen's and Sera's searches are pointed at the same goddess from opposite directions.
14. **The Aether.** Nobody knows; Seris calls it "the root".

## Questions the docs don't answer (worth asking the author before writing forward)

- **"Valory"** appears once, in Ch 3 ("He felt the vow. He felt Valory."). Is it the sister's name, the village, or a leftover?
- Varen's sister is never named and neither is his village. Deliberate?
- What are the two remaining protagonists' real names? (Aria and Auriander are placeholders, and the author has said names may change.)
- Who broke Caen's sword and whose was it?
- What is Ryn's real past, and is the gold-haired woman alive?
- What do three pulses mean, and who sent them?
- What was the Godless Hand's actual target in the Capital?
- Does the six-protagonist group ever meet as one, and when?
- Sera's nature has no name yet. Is it meant to stay unnamed?
