# Continuity Flags

Things I noticed while reading all three docs. None of them are fixed; they're listed so the author can decide.

## Numbers and facts that disagree

1. **The bounty is two gold or three gold.** The original manuscript Ch 10 says *two gold*, and Ch 17 and 19 agree ("You nearly died for two gold"). The **1st Revision raised it to three gold** in Ch 10 and nowhere else, so the revision now contradicts the later chapters. Either roll Ch 10 back to two, or raise the number in Ch 17 and 19 when they're revised.
2. **Corrupter count.** Ch 14–17 as written: one scouts the camp, one attacks first, then five more come, and six paws are taken. Mara says "It's six actually". The count works, but the first creature to scout and the first to attack read as the same animal in some passages, so it's worth a check when revising.
3. **Sera's age when orphaned.** The manuscript says her mother died when she was **four**; the notes say she was orphaned at **five** and picked up at six. The church intake at six is consistent.
4. **Rifles distributed.** Ch 18 says Vorne walks out with **three guards** carrying rifles, and the notes correct this explicitly: only **three rifles** were made, because the military smiths concentrated on crossbows. One earlier passage implies ten rifles for ten best soldiers ("One hundred rounds… ten allocated to each of his ten best soldiers"). The notes' version (three rifles) is the later decision.
5. **Wand name.** Project memory says **Wand of Frosting**; the manuscript says **Wand of Rime**. Manuscript wins unless the author says otherwise.
6. **Severin's gender in the project's character doc** was wrong (listed as female). Severin is the male wind mage working with Elysia. Fixed in the updated docs.
7. **Haes** is a lieutenant of the *northern* brigade, not one of Elysia's people, which is another thing the old character doc had backwards.
8. **Highmore and Emmeline as names on the gate log.** The "pilgrims" gave their real names to the guards, which Vorne reads out. That's a deliberate arrogance, but worth confirming it's intended, since Cedric later says he'd hoped Vorne would be "with Emmeline and Highmore".
9. **Guides count.** Ch 8 says twenty-three Guides arrive and the room empties "twenty-three becoming twenty-two, twenty-one, twenty…". Fine, but the senior (Caen) is one of them, so the total is 23 including him.
10. **Ryn's memory span.** Stated as five years consistently, but Ch 13 says "as far as he could remember, which was five years, which was the extent of the back there was", while Ch 7 (revision) implies he has been trading across multiple kingdoms for a while. Compatible, just tight.

## Chapter numbering and headings

- **Ch 16's in-doc heading says "Chapter Fourteen — The Cabin Between Worlds 2."** Should be Sixteen.
- The project's `claude/chapter-index.md` lists "16 The Training Ground" and "17 The Cabin Between Worlds 2", which is off by one. **The Training Ground is a section inside Ch 15**, not its own chapter. Corrected index is in this folder and has been written back to the project.
- Ch 13 has a stray `### ` heading with no title in the middle of the chapter.
- The notes doc labels the Blighted Murel fight as "Chapter Nineteen", while in the manuscript it's Ch 21 ("What the Well Kept"). The notes numbering is one or two behind the manuscript's tabs from Ch 19 onward.

## Editing artifacts left in the manuscript

- **Ch 12** contains an assistant's chat text pasted into the prose: "Got it — back to Chapter 12, that closing passage." followed by bullet-style notes ("**The corruption holding answers**…", "Here's the revised closing passage:"). Roughly ten lines that need deleting.
- **Ch 20 / Ch 15** have duplicated sentences left in: "He moved through it the way he moved through all markets…" appears twice in a row, "Vorne came down from his office with two men behind him" appears twice, "The students looked at him" appears twice, "Solen held it for another second. Then he laughed" appears twice, and "He threw it." appears twice in Ch 21.
- **Ch 22** has a stray "23 -" in the middle of a line at the western gate.
- A few passages still carry `(insert name)` style placeholders and parenthetical instructions, mostly in the notes doc rather than the manuscript.
- Formatting is inconsistent: some chapters wrap all dialogue in italics/asterisks and some don't. Ch 14 onward drifts into italic blocks for whole scenes.

## Terminology to sweep in revision

- **mana → arcana**, **life force → anima** everywhere except where the church speaks (divine energy / Providence's blessing), and nobody says **Aether** except Seris's private "the root". Chapters 1–25 still use the old words throughout; the Ch 26 notes already use "arcana".
- "Magister" is the address; "sorceress of the northern valley" is the outside reputation.
- Creature names to keep consistent: **Corrupter**, **World Feeder**, **Mureling / Murel**, **Blighted Murel**, **Sangui-batrachos**.
