## Writing workflow
1. Party sends a scene prompt with what happens, including exact dialogue.
2. Claude breaks the beats and critiques the prompt's storytelling.
3. Party gives the all-clear.
4. Claude writes the scene — dialogue unchanged in tone and content (vocabulary may be adjusted to fit period and theme).
5. Never get ahead of the author.
