## THE SIX PROTAGONISTS
Four are on the page: **Varen, Mara, Ryn, Sera**. Two more join later and both start as enemies of Varen: **Aria**, a dryad, and **Auriander**, a lunatic cultist (working names; from project memory, not yet in the docs).

### Varen
- Age 20 now. Village burned when he was 8; two years feral; found by Caen at 10; ten years of training; Caen left at 20.
- Ash-white hair (the colour stopped from the inside out), dark eyes that settle rather than dart, lean the way a blade is lean, forearms and hands heavily scarred, calloused past skin.
- Wears the direwolf hide he killed at 10. Carries a hunting knife (melted by acid in Ch 19, replaced for a while by Mara's small knife) and **Caen's broken sword**, snapped at one deliberate point to shortsword length, restored blade, neglected hilt.
- Heals unnaturally fast, reads terrain and tracks like nothing else, moves without waste, throws a rock accurately at absurd distance. His **life force pushes his physical body past normal limits** (author's note), and flashbacks can trigger short surges of strength.
- Speaks flat and literally, files what he doesn't understand instead of pressing, and can't lie; he tells his first lie in Ch 17 to protect Mara's feelings.
- His life force reads as **blue fire** and has a ceiling no envoy has been able to find.
- The vow: **"I'll burn them."** He wants Yharim, and by Ch 14 he's also considering finding Providence.
- Wound he doesn't look at: his sister asked "I want to go with you" and he said "This isn't a girl's job."

### Mara
- Nine years old, small for nine, brown hair tied back, quick dark eyes. Ordenmere-born (west district), raised by the street.
- Thief and liar by trade, with practised fake memories (Garan and the fruit stall) and stories that always end with someone on a roof. Reads faces professionally.
- Owned by **Alister**, who takes her earnings and hurts her when the take is low. She sleeps in a crawl space under a market stall.
- Counts money in her sleep. Obsessed with gold-to-silver-to-copper conversions.
- Moves at height better than anyone, which is decisive in the Blighted Murel fight, where the plan is hers.
- Arc so far: she starts running a long con on Varen and ends up staying when staying costs her. "I'm going to get stronger. I won't be in your way anymore."

### Ryn Casvel
- Trader, inventor, entrepreneur, and (though he denies it) a very good alchemist: "Just a humble pyrotechnician."
- Dark skin, clean-shaven, medium build, deep brown coat fitted precisely, easy eyes that register everything. A performance smile and a real one.
- **Has only five years of memory.** He gets fragments: a spring field, a tree, warmth, and a **gold-haired woman** who says "You're making that face again. You're thinking too much." In Ch 21 she says instead **"What have you done to me."** The fragments fire near Sera, and Ch 11 notes that only a wall separated him from sleeping Varen when the longest one came.
- His **life force empowers his intelligence** (author's note). Later he will find the world's only **philosopher stone** and unlock an alchemy that doesn't obey this world's rules.
- Invented gunpowder-equivalent chemistry (suevite-based propellant), the **revolver** (a 1847 Colt Walker in the author's head), a magazine-fed double-spring **repeating crossbow**, and a bolt-action **rifle**. Sells schematics, never ammunition.
- Derived the third law of motion himself. Works with a five-year **field journal** in his own notation.
- Fair dealer; doesn't negotiate a fair price. Treats people as variables, which is why Sera unsettles him.

### Sera
- No family name. Orphaned young (four in the manuscript, five in the notes), taken by the church at six, 18 years a nun, ten of them in the capital and eight at the western border.
- Gold hair, and the warmth in her survives everything, which is what Ryn can't stop noticing.
- Purification and minor healing. Her cowl is uniquely bright and warm, burns without heat, is **viscous** in a way no other mana is, and **doesn't dissipate; it rises and lingers.** Seris can't reproduce it. It's the closest thing yet to the quality (not quantity) of mana that made the ancient artifacts.
- Left the church after the World Feeder morning and the alley prayer that got no answer. Now Seris's student and research assistant.
- Goal: find out whether her sisters' deaths mattered, and find Providence herself.
- She still believes. What she rejected was the institution.

## MENTORS AND POWERS

### Caen / Signus
- A **Guide** (envoy) of the **Devourer of Gods**, and the senior of the 23. His mortal form is deliberately unremarkable. His real name is **Signus**.
- Took Varen at 10 and spent twelve years building him. He hasn't reported a harvest in years, which is what draws the Weaver's attention.
- Voice flat and observational; asks questions he knows the answer to. The **wide grin** appears when something he's been working toward arrives.
- Calls Varen "little animal", first as a label, later as warmth. Left him the broken sword and no note.
- Not cruel and not gentle. He tends Varen through two months of fever, feeds his campfire in secret, and will kill anyone who threatens the plan.
- Has a plan for Varen he has never explained to anyone, including the Devourer. Whatever it is, it isn't food.

### Seris Dahl (Magister, western brigade)
- Dark hair, expensive structured clothing, shoes that announce her. Two-word corrections end conversations.
- "**Dahl**" is a name she manifested; she has no family. Known abroad as the **sorceress of the northern valley**, a reputation the Godless Hand recognize instantly.
- Twenty years hunting lost history: ancient languages, erased civilizations, **runes that store mana**, and enchanted artifacts. In Ordenmere two years on a deal, teaching in exchange for capital-library access. The library gave her nothing; a children's book gave her everything.
- Teaching method: assess in a glance, tailor pressure per student (validation for Bael, silence for Myre), never lower the bar.
- Water nature, and far past her students' ceiling: an effortless water blade cuts deeper into stone than Ryn's rifle. She can produce other natures, which takes years. In the Ch 26 notes she achieves **Convergence** and switches her cowl to wind.
- Central insight (Ch 22): mages measure **quantity** of mana because it's all they can measure. Sera's shows there's **quality**.

### Aldous Vorne (Captain, western brigade)
- Huge, greatsword on his back, everything on his desk with a purpose. Grieved enough and can't be broken.
- Detective instincts: he reads a gate log and reconstructs the whole infiltration.
- Bought Ryn's schematics and armed his best men. Goes red whenever Seris addresses him, which the students find hilarious.
- Ch 25: broken greatsword, stabbed in the gut by Cedric's hidden steel, and he snaps Cedric's elbow.

### Aldric
- Dwarf blacksmith by the western wall. Huge, worked hands, a face that gives nothing away.
- Built a mechanical forced-air system into his forge (pipe in the wall, steam vents, no bellows), which is why his fire burns white-bright and constant.
- Reads a blueprint and knows what it's for: "It's designed to withstand a blast. I would know."
- Building a **clock escapement**. He and Ryn think at the same speed. Open engineering problem: wear at the pallet contact points causes drift (the real-world answer is jewel bearings, which Ryn hasn't reached yet).

### Maret
- Supervising nun, 40 years at the border, 30 since she chose faith over looking into the rot herself. No cruelty left in her, just the smooth hard surface the work leaves.
- Gave Sera her purse and her blessing, and pointed her west without naming Seris.

### Father Ignatius
- 73, thin, white hair swept back, pale cold-blue eyes, deliberate movements. Runs the western district chapel.
- "Your failures to carry her will are yours. And yours only." Capable of an instantaneous, total rage that departs as fast as it arrives.

## THE GODLESS HAND (attacking Ordenmere)
They refuse a name: *"We don't give ourselves a name really. Why would we. After all we leave nothing behind but the rubble."* They call themselves **the enlightened**, carry out **"his will"** (the Tyrant's, i.e. Yharim's), and are "enough to attack three kingdoms in one night." Farewell: **"Let the deep void claim you, brother. We will meet again in the eldritch."**

- **Elysia** — fire. Tall, blonde, black eyes that barely blink, forehead covered in dense tattooed prayer: *"Tyrant, make of this land a place kept clean of false light. Keep me and what I love from kneeling to what was stolen and called divine."* Sustained flame rather than bursts, recites verses over the dead, goes quiet after burning people. Wields an **ancient four-page fire book**.
- **Severin** — wind/compressed air, flight. Lean, forties, dark hair greying at the temples, close-trimmed goatee. Cruel and amused; feels almost no pain (took a crossbow bolt out of his shoulder joint without reacting). Works with Elysia in wordless sync.
- **Aurelius Highmore** — compressed air, invisible and lethal, and a self-propelled charge. Forties, handsome, dark hair, black eyes, dark robes. **House Highmore of Oakhaven**, nearly started a war twenty years ago. Former student of **Balthazar Astorel** and his murderer, plus Balthazar's wife and children. Hates the word "heretic". Talks too much. Soft spot for young mages.
- **Emmeline** — pure cold (no moisture, not ice as anyone knows it). Tall, brown waist-length braid, precise. Wields the **Wand of Rime**: an iced branch with a blue gem that creates ice instantly from every point at once, and leaves *real* water behind.
- **Cedric** — earth and **quartzite** armour/weapons, and a close-quarters fighter built for it. Enjoys the fight, talkative, genuinely devoted. Hidden trick: a plain steel sword behind his quartzite vambrace. Loses Silas and grieves him.
- **Silas** — light (blinding release, beams) and **summoning**, which is his life's work. Thin, pale, young, flat dry humour, flies a **broom** artifact. Summons shadow ravens and the **Sangui-batrachos**. Summons feed on his **life force**, not mana, and damage to a summon costs him directly. Killed by Berrick with Lucian's rifle.

**Their plan that night:** infiltrate in pairs on different days through different gates, take the northern compound (Elysia/Severin), take the western compound (Highmore/Emmeline), and hold the southern gate (Cedric/Silas) with beacon signals to coordinate. Target: something beyond the western district, reachable only by the spiral through every district. It went wrong because Vorne is paranoid, the chapel was alerted, and Seris happened to be in Ordenmere.

## MILITARY, CHURCH AND CITY
- **Lieutenant Haes** — northern brigade. Big and dense, black braided hair, one eye (old scar), early thirties. Held the compound and died burned kneeling with sword and shield in hand.
- **Harold** — Haes's right hand; distributed the crossbows.
- **Sir Tristan Castellan** — Vorne's steady knight; fingers crushed by Silas, pinned under the toad.
- **Sir Berrick Morvayn** — fastest sword hand Vorne ever fought beside; killed Silas with Lucian's rifle; killed by Cedric's boulder.
- **Sir Lucian Hallow** — youngest, quiet, always positioned right; killed by Silas's ravens. His rifle killed Silas and was then shattered by Cedric.
- **Corren and Aldun** — the gate guards who let "the pilgrims" in. **Gaveth** — western gate guard who recognises Mara. **Petch** and **Corbyn** — Voss's bandits.
- **Clerics:** **Leonard** (flight; abandoned the border team, left to die by his own brothers), **Tobias** (water, the tactician of the three), **Julian** (ice), **Gideon** (light spears), **Valerius** (fire; cauterizes his own arm), **Hadrian** (earth, dust streams), **Mathias** (stone), plus **Cyrus** and **Dorian** in the chapel.
- **Nuns:** **Lise** (16, sincere, "It must be beautiful to die doing Providence's will"), **Yel** (killed by the World Feeder), **Wenna** (face destroyed by acid).
- **Alister** — pleasant-voiced man who runs Mara and other child thieves; has moles among the guards.
- **Voss** — feared bandit leader, killed by Ryn. **Corbyn** — his reluctant successor and Ryn's client.

## COSMIC / THE ORDER
- **The Devourer of Gods ("dog")** — consumes the **auric souls** Yharim thought were being destroyed, and is fed cultivated life force by the Order. The chain of authority runs through it.
- **The Weaver** — serpentine, enormous, **platinum-silver scales**, moves in curves. Supervises the Order, forced his way up during the war, and has given the Devourer nothing since. Caen's threat lands hard because it's true. Now runs Lyris as his own informant.
- **Lyris** — envoy. Meticulous, ambitious, politically smart. Found Varen at 14 and couldn't read his ceiling; saw the blue fire in the inn; nearly killed by Caen; now a double agent reporting to the Weaver only what Caen permits. Cultivating a village boy, **Brask** (raw capacity, no direction yet).
- **Maren** (eastern settlements, oldest embedding) and **Sael** (coastal) — other envoys who report normally.

## MENTIONED, NOT YET ON THE PAGE
- **Yharim** — the Tyrant. The boy from the magma; killed the gods; withdrew into the jungle. The name the envoys are forbidden to speak. Varen intends to find him.
- **Yharon** — the **dragon of rebirth**, hatched in the magma, pulled Yharim out by choice. He died once and came back; his soul merged with Yharim's while both stayed separate consciousnesses.
- **Balthazar Astorel** — unlocked wind magic's true potential; killed by his student Highmore.
- **Providence, the Profaned Goddess** — possibly the last living goddess. Never answers.
- **Silva**, first dryad — held the philosopher stone; died in the Abyss.
- **Calamitas** — created brimstone flame to rival Providence's flame (or the dragon of flames). One of the exceptional mortals.
- **The big five** and **Draedon** — other mortals whose life force pushed them past normal limits (see `03-world-and-lore.md`).
