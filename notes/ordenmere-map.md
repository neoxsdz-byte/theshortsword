# Ordenmere — Kingdom Geography

## OVERVIEW
A walled kingdom spanning 15km × 15km, built in a plus/cross shape with five districts around a central capital. Total enclosed area including the three passage zones: ~187.5km².

## THE CAPITAL
- Dead centre, a 5km × 5km walled square (25km²).
- One gate only — **Gate 10** — connecting exclusively to the Northern District from the north.
- No direct gates from the Capital to the Western, Eastern or Southern Districts.
- The Northern District is the mandatory chokepoint for entering or leaving the seat of power.

## THE FOUR DISTRICTS (each 5km × 5km = 25km²)

**Northern District** (above the Capital) — Gate 9 eastern wall; Gate 10 southern wall (down into the Capital); western wall plain, no gate.
**Western District** (left) — Gate 1 northern wall; Gate 2 western outer wall; Gate 3 southern wall.
**Eastern District** (right, borders the Great River) — Gate 8 northern wall; Gate 6 southern wall; Gate 7 eastern wall, the drawbridge onto Lord Shaemus Bridge.
**Southern District** (below) — Gate 4 western wall; Gate 5 eastern wall.

## THE THREE PASSAGE ZONES
Three of the four outer corners are enclosed by diagonal walls running 7.07km, each sealing a right-triangular zone with 5km legs and 12.5km² of interior. Walled enclosures, not open gaps. Cramped and poor — the slums.

- **North East Passage** — between Northern (Gate 9) and Eastern (Gate 8).
- **South East Passage** — between Eastern (Gate 6) and Southern (Gate 5); the diagonal angles toward the bridge approach.
- **South West Passage** — between Southern (Gate 4) and Western (Gate 3).
- **North West corner** — NO passage. Northern and Western meet at a plain square outer corner. Gate 1 is the closest gate.

## THE GREAT RIVER
Runs the entire eastern edge, 1.65km wide. Only crossing is **Lord Shaemus Bridge**, aligned with Gate 7 — fixed stone arches the whole way except one liftable section at the wall. The far bank is wilderness. Sole land connection to whatever lies east.

## THE ROYAL ROAD IS A SPIRAL
In through the west, then West → South → East → North, and finally into the Capital through its single northern gate. Every district, every passage, in sequence. No shortcut, no direct route. **The kingdom is the security system** — this is what Vorne reasons from in Ch 22.

## DISTRICT CHARACTER
West loud and mercantile, and the most militarised (the western brigade compound sits against the wall). North forges and smoke. East docks and fish. South fields and windmills. The Capital has no outer wall, no river access and no windows on the world; it holds the Royal Palace and the Church of Providence, with a branch chapel in each district.
