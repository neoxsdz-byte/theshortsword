## How to read the references

Paragraph numbers count non-blank paragraphs from the start of each chapter. The chapter heading itself is ¶0, so Ch12 ¶347 is the 347th paragraph of text after the Chapter Twelve heading.

Chapter numbers are by position in the document, not by what the heading says. The 16th chapter is called Ch 16 here even though its heading currently reads “Chapter Fourteen” — see B1.

Deleting a paragraph shifts every number below it in that chapter. Work from the bottom of a chapter upward, or just search the quoted text.

“Revision” means 1st Revision.docx, which is canon for Ch 1–10. “Manuscript” means The Shortsword.docx.

## A. Contradictions

### A1 — The bounty is two gold in some chapters and three in others — and one of Ryn's lines depends on which

**Where:** Revision Ch10 ¶108 and ¶112; Manuscript Ch10 ¶109 and ¶113; Manuscript Ch17 ¶256, ¶282, ¶284, ¶286, ¶295; Ch19 ¶324

The revision settled Ch 10 on three gold and made it consistent inside the chapter:

**Revision Ch10 ¶108** *“Three gold, she said. For a corrupted presence southwest of the walls.”*

**Revision Ch10 ¶112** *“Three previous attempts. Three gold — not desperate money, official money…”*

The old manuscript contradicts itself inside that same chapter:

**Manuscript Ch10 ¶109** *“Three Gold, she said.”*

**Manuscript Ch10 ¶113** *“Two gold — not desperate money, official money…”*

And Ch 17 and 19 are still built on two:

**Ch17 ¶284** *“Two gold. For what five of those.”*

**Ch17 ¶286** *“You nearly died, Ryn said, for two gold.”*

**Ch19 ¶324** *“you took this commission for two gold and you didn't negotiate it up.”*

**The part that actually bites:** Ch17 ¶295 is Ryn's offer: “I'll pay six gold. Three times what you came for.” Six is three times two. If the bounty becomes three gold, that line is wrong — six is twice three. So raising the number means either changing six to nine, or changing “three times” to “twice”. This is the one place where the number isn't just a number.

**Don't confuse it with:** Ch19 ¶38–45 and Ch21 ¶180 also say “three gold”, but that's Ryn's advance to Mara out of her six, not the bounty. Those are correct as they stand.

**Options:** (a) Keep three gold and fix Ch17 ¶256/¶282/¶284/¶286 and Ch19 ¶324, plus the arithmetic in Ch17 ¶295. (b) Roll the revision back to two gold in Ch10 ¶108 and ¶112 and change nothing else.
