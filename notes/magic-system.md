# Magic System Reference

> **Status: partly superseded.** This file was written in August 2026, before the full manuscript read. `world-and-lore.md` is the current and more complete account of the magic system, and corrects three things below. Three further errors were found on the 10 Oct read and are marked **[corrected]** inline. This file still needs a proper rewrite — flagged, not done, because that's an author decision.

## TERMINOLOGY
- **Arcana** — what all mages call their magical energy (replaces "mana" going forward).
- **Anima** — the vital energy of living things, formerly "life force". Only the envoys of the Devourer and Caen know the term. Secret knowledge.
- **Aether** — the root source of arcana. Nobody knows of it; Seris suspects it and calls it **"the root"**.
- The church still says "divine energy" or "Providence's blessing".
- Ch 1–25 still use the old terms. See `continuity-flags.md` section E for the exact per-chapter counts: 132 "mana", 24 "life force".

## ARCANA
- Sits in the space around everyone; absorbed through the skin.
- Church method: absorb, push out through the palms. Seris's method: absorb, let it stay, distribute through the whole body (the **cowl**).
- Capability depends on the *relationship* between caster and arcana — how far the mind and body understand and accept it.
- Anything conjured from arcana returns to arcana. Nothing elemental is permanent.

## THE COWL
Visible field of arcana held through the whole body. Colour and behaviour show the caster's **nature**; quality shows their character. Years of casting through the hands leaves a groove that has to be unlearned.

- **Fire** (Bael) — red, aggressive, largest output; concentrates in the hands.
- **Earth** (Iren) — green, calm, patient; too conservative, won't take it in with the whole body.
- **Wind** (Davan) — light, fast, irregular pulses rather than steady flow.
- **Water** (Solen) — the most even distribution; bleeds at the edges.
- **Lightning** (Myre) — sharp, precise; binary, collapses instantly. Later produces ball lightning.
- **Sera** — bright, warm, burns without heat, illuminates without being light. **[corrected]** Not "purification/light". Seris explicitly rules light out in Ch 18: it resembles fire more than anything, it is **viscous**, and it **rises instead of dissipating**. It has no name yet. Her cowl also does not "drain fast" — by Ch 20 it is the largest and steadiest in the group.

Training ladder as Seris teaches it: hold the cowl → hold it evenly → hold it effortlessly → **funnel** (release outward while intake continues) → manifest the element → **shift nature (Convergence)**.

## ANIMA — SECRET KNOWLEDGE
- Distinct from arcana. Everyone has one; almost nobody knows it exists. It is the individual's existence itself, and it sets the ceiling of the vessel: body, intelligence, understanding.
- **Nature comes from anima** — a mage's element isn't chosen or trained in, it expresses what they are.
- Spending anima costs lifespan and recovers slowly. Spend past what remains and you die.
- Readable by those with the awareness. Has age, density and signature. Anima from a worn object (the wolf hide) seeps faintly into the wearer over years.
- **[corrected]** It was **Lyris**, not the Weaver, who read Varen's anima at fourteen and again in the inn. The Weaver only heard her report.
- Varen's reads as **blue fire**, dense and refined far past his age, with a ceiling no envoy could find.

## SUMMONING
- Summoners don't create. Minions are pulled from another dimension via **runic magic**; the summoner binds his soul to the minion as a contract, and that bind is the rent.
- **Cost:** summons feed on the summoner's **anima**, and damage to a summon is charged to the summoner directly.
- Minions can't really die while summoned — they regenerate on the summoner's anima. Kill the summoner and they return. So the fight is never about destroying the summon; it's the summoner's clock or reaching the summoner. Ch 24–25 plays exactly this way: nobody kills the toad, Berrick shoots Silas.
- **[corrected]** Silas's summoning circle appears **above his head** (with a second on the ground for the toad), not on his forehead.
- The **Sangui-batrachos**: huge red toad, absorbs bullets and bolts without reaction, tongue as primary weapon, vulnerable to fire inside the mouth, croak audible across the district. Dismissed completely and instantly.

## RUNES AND ENCHANTED ARTIFACTS
- A script in our own letters that nobody can read, predating every current religion, treated with reverence in surviving fragments.
- Runes **store and self-supply** arcana — some pull from ambient air, others convert it into the effect. No operator needed, unlike the church's **pylons**, which need people feeding them constantly.
- Seris's exact replica of the lantern fails. Her conclusion in Ch 22: the makers used arcana of a different **quality**, not a greater quantity.
- Known artifacts: the **self-powered lantern**; the **Wand of Rime** (Emmeline's iced branch — ice erupts from every point simultaneously and leaves real water, "breaks every fundamental rule"); the **four-page fire book** (fire that clings like oil and eats a barrier's mana; pages turn themselves); **Silas's broom** (loses weight when fed arcana).

## HEALING / PURIFICATION
- The church's "purification" is a branch of **healing magic**, predating the church, with nothing to do with Providence, and one of the hardest forms of arcana manipulation there is. Anyone can learn it regardless of nature; it takes years and many fail.
- Works on living tissue at the level of its smallest parts: distinguish damaged from healthy, instruct, sustain the instruction without disrupting what still works.
- Sera trained from six to sixteen before being trusted at the border. "If it can heal it can also destroy."

## RIFLES ARE NOT MAGIC
Ryn's weapons take no arcana and don't respond to it, which is what makes them unclassifiable to mages. Silas fed his arcana into a stolen rifle and got nothing.

## THE CORRUPTION
A dead god's flesh and blood, spreading where it fell. It advances, learns and adapts, and moves underground where purification can't reach. Forty years ago the border was three weeks west of Ordenmere's wall; now it's hours. The church calls it the **Unmaking** and teaches divine punishment — mostly invention that grew from a footnote into the centrepiece.

Creatures: **Corrupters** (hunt by sound, pack-linked; a skull strike deafens one and the pack tears it apart), **World Feeders** (burrow, only move forward, hunt by vibration, acid that melts stone and steel), **Blighted Murel** (twenty fused Murelings, nerve cluster at the core, reabsorbs its own mass above all else).
