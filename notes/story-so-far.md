## ARC 1: The Animal (Varen's origin)

### Ch 1: The Animal and The Name
Varen is 10. He has lived feral in the snowy forest for two years since his village burned. His hair has gone flat ash-white and he no longer speaks. While he's gutting a rabbit, an unremarkable man (Caen) takes his knife without effort, knocks him down, grabs him by the neck and walks him back through the ruins of his village to his own burned house. In the corner lie the remains of his mother, his father, and "the small shape" (his little sister). Caen keeps demanding "WHAT ARE YOU?" until Varen gets out "I'm Varen." Caen grins and asks what he would do if he saw who was responsible. **"I'll burn them."** Caen walks away and Varen follows.

### Ch 2: The Knife and The Cold
Varen follows Caen into the deep forest. Caen drops the knife in the snow in front of a direwolf standing on a fresh deer kill and taunts him: "Are you scared of predators?… I thought you were going to burn them." Varen fights the wolf in a long, brutal fight. His knee is dislocated, his arm broken, his ribs shifted, but his hand never opens and the wolf dies. He wakes in Caen's cabin after about two months of fever, with his wounds set and wrapped. Caen asks, "How long are you going to sleep, little animal", and this time the words are warm. Varen stays.

### Ch 3: The Hunt That Walks
Varen is 14, three years with Caen, wearing the wolf's hide. He hunts and butchers alone, and Caen leaves for weeks at a time. Caen takes him to a bandit camp ("a little animal that thinks itself a predator") and hands him the knife: "Hunt." Varen hesitates ("He didn't do anything to me." / "The wolf didn't do anything to you either. Until it did.") and takes the man down. The man begs while reaching for his sword, and Varen cuts his throat. Caen has silently killed the other three. Caen rages: "DO YOU THINK A SNAKE LOSES ITS VENOM IF IT DOESN'T BITE ANYMORE?… WHAT ARE YOU?" Varen answers **"I am Varen,"** understanding that the name and the vow are the same thing. (Note: one line says "He felt Valory." The word appears only here.)

### Ch 4: The Name at the End of the Line
By a dying fire Caen tells the story of **Yharim** without naming him yet: a 12-year-old boy thrown into magma holding an egg. His hands never opened. The egg hatched (**Yharon**), and the hatchling pulled the burned boy up to a jungle floor, both of them "pointed" at the same target. Then the crusade: the gods fell one by one. Two gods' remains weren't dealt with properly, and they spread into **the Corruption** and **the Crimson**. From that wreckage came **the Godless Hand**, true believers who think the crusade must continue by burning anyone who still worships. Varen now understands what he saw at eight: a figure floating over his burning village, face hidden by fire, reciting from memory: *"Thus it is written in the ash of the old gods. Those who kneel before false divinity shall know the fire that does not distinguish… Their flesh shall bear witness to what their faith could not… Flesh that burns in our fire burns clean. We are its mercy. We do not cease."* He ran and didn't stop for two years.

### Ch 5: The Knife Hand
Varen is 20, after ten years of training. He's lean and white-haired, with dark eyes that settle on things, and he's covered in scars. In the clearing he spars with Caen and for the first time forces him to take a real step, using the root on the eastern side. Caen then goes after the knife hand, grip, nerve strike and all, but the hand stays closed. That night the fire burns higher than it needs to. In the morning Caen is gone for good, and Varen **never touched him once in ten years**. Left on the table is a **broken sword** that has been cleanly snapped at a single deliberate point, restored to shortsword length with a sharp edge, but with a worn grip and a loose guard. No note. Varen packs the hide, knife and sword, and walks.

### Ch 6: The Road and The Little Thief
**Part 1, The Road.** Varen heads north along the Corruption's edge, which has always been his landmark. He cuts down large corruption monsters easily. He's met no people in twelve years except ones he killed. He finds **seven dead adventurers** and reconstructs the fight. They had windows to escape and chose to stay together and die together, and he can't explain it ("the gap").
**Part 2, The Little Thief.** A 9-year-old brown-haired girl, **Mara**, tries to pick his pocket, and he catches her wrist. She's terrified, and he's confused because she fits no category he has. She spins a lie about being separated from a group heading north. He believes it: "I'm also going north." She attaches herself to him. She knows the answer to his "gap" question but can't say it, because nobody has ever stayed for her.

### Ch 7: The Trader
A well-dressed, dark-skinned man (**Ryn**, not named in this chapter) walks deliberately into bandit territory outside Ordenmere. He's captured and brought to **Voss**, a feared bandit chief ("Noble?" / "Trader.", "I sell death."). He produces a **revolver** and shoots Voss dead. He then makes the second-in-command, **Corbyn**, his client: he hands him an unloaded revolver, laughs off Corbyn's attempt to shoot him ("Slow down, big guy… you're a silly one, Gerald"), and puts a round through a live oak beside his head. The deal takes 11 minutes: Corbyn pays on a schedule, Ryn supplies the ammunition, and without Ryn there are no bullets. Walking back, Ryn gets a memory fragment (warmth, a color, something held) that he files under *not now, not here, not useful*. "The file was getting thick."

### Ch 8: The Cabin Between Worlds
**Part 1, The Meeting.** In the void, Caen conjures a copy of his cabin. **23 Guides** (the Devourer's envoys wearing forgettable human forms) give their harvest reports: Maren (eastern settlements), Sael (coastal), and others. Caen doesn't report. **Lyris** gives a flawless report, then asks publicly how long it's been since "our senior" reported a meal to the Devourer. Caen realises she's been in his cabin and seen Varen. "Your concern is noted… You may sit."
**Part 2, The Void.** A dark, serpentine voice that supervises "the Order" confronts Caen, who says the chain of authority doesn't pass through it. "We'll see about that." Caen goes back to the real, cold cabin, sits in the cold ash, and holds it in his hand.
**Part 3, The Summoning.** The voice summons Lyris. She had watched 14-year-old Varen for seven days and couldn't find the ceiling of his life force; it wasn't meant for the Devourer. For a moment she glimpses **platinum-silver scales moving in a curve** (the Weaver). Her orders: "Find the boy. Observe. Report to me." And about Caen: "**Signus** is not your concern."

### Ch 9: The Edge of Things
**Part 1, The Walls.** Mara talks nonstop, mostly stories that end with someone on a roof (Garan and the fruit stall). She constructs fake memories so well she half believes them. Varen can't see the walls she points at ("Your eyesight is terrible." / "Yes."), turns on her word, and the walls appear exactly where she said. At the gate a guard, **Gaveth**, recognises Mara. She lies them through. In the market a boy chasing a girl triggers Varen's first warm flash of his sister. "What's rent." Mara pays for his room with coins taken off the seven dead.
**Part 2, First Night.** Mara doesn't have a room. She sleeps in a crawl space under a market stall. Her master, **Alister**, a pleasant-voiced, controlling man who runs child thieves, is waiting and takes her small take ("My other workers made this in an hour"). It's implied he beats her. Varen dreams he's 8, racing his 7-year-old sister home, and for the first time looks directly at the corner of the room. He wakes with the verse and the corner together and a settled certainty that the path exists. **Lyris**, in the room, watches his life force flare into **deep blue fire** for about 30 seconds and briefly feels the lower edge of his ceiling, far above her. She smiles. Something platinum moves between worlds with the excitement of very interesting news.

### Ch 10: The Commission Board
Varen eats jerked meat and meets Mara in the corridor ("Did you eat?"). He clocks her deflection ("All kingdoms look the same anyway"). A contained explosion sounds from the military compound (Ryn's weapon tests) and Varen files it as significant. At the commission board he sees Mara is guarding her left arm and publicly offers to tend it. "I'M FINE." The job she picks: **three gold** for a corrupted presence southwest of the walls, with three failed attempts, the same creatures that killed the seven. He tells her not to come. She runs out of practical arguments: "I want to go with you." He flashes to 8 years old, heading into the woods with an axe and telling his sister "This isn't a girl's job," the last time he saw her. "Fine." At the western gate they pass the returning **border nuns** and a carriage of covered bodies.

## ARC 2: Ordenmere (the city threads converge)

### Ch 11: The Thunder Inside the Walls
Ryn enters the west district through the northern gate as a "trade commission weapons consultant". He buys acid wood bark and anechoic coating for **one vertebra** from Crimson territory, explaining what one actually costs to get, and pulls out the hidden **vile mushrooms**. He commissions parts from three blacksmiths. The third is the dwarf **Aldric**, whose forge runs on a hidden forced-air mechanism instead of bellows. Aldric deliberately introduces one tolerance error to show he knows the part must "withstand a blast", then fixes it: "I would know." They trade names ("Aldric. The blacksmith." / "Ryn. The trader."). That night Ryn builds with **infernal suevite** (volcanic glass from the Pyre) and a viscous gel. He dreams of a **gold-haired woman under a spring tree**: "You're making that face again. You're thinking too much." (He's in the room next to sleeping Varen: "three feet of wall… separated him from the answer.") At the western brigade compound he demonstrates to **Captain Aldous Vorne** a ten-bolt double-spring **repeating crossbow**. **Seris Dahl** arrives with five students. One student (**Bael**) incinerates a target to put down a soldier who asked "Is this a magic item?": "Worm." Ryn: "Say, worm… what if I told you that you could be as powerful as a mage?" A soldier fires Ryn's **rifle** and the round cratered the stone wall behind the target. Seris ejects the casing and smells it: "Suevite… It's not combustible… How does it fit in your recipe." / "Trade secret." Deal: 20 gold for the crossbow schematics, 1 platinum for the rifle schematics, 100 rounds to start, then 30 silver per round. Ryn keeps the ammunition to himself.

### Ch 12: A Day in the Life of a Nun
Sera's border team (16 sisters, 8 clerics, a supervising nun) purifies the rot before dawn: centimeters won back while it advances by meters underground. Sister **Yel** (Sera knew her name against the rule) is bitten in half by something unseen. A **World Feeder** (a burrowing worm with a toothed ring mouth, acid-slick skin, and it only moves forward) hunts by vibration. A cleric's lightning kills a sister, the fifth cleric dies ordering the team to spread, a cleric runs away and dies, and the wind cleric (**Leonard**) flies off and abandons them. "It only finds us when we move." Sera freezes and doesn't cross twenty steps to save a bleeding sister. The clerics finally trap it with ice, fire and light spears. Sera's question changes from *is this your will* to **"Where are you."** Her purification removes the rot but not the **red** of the blood. Back inside, the children revere them and Sera feels like she's wearing a dead woman's clothes. A 16-year-old novice, **Lise**, asks about funerals (there are none; they're all orphans) and says **"It must be beautiful to die doing Providence's will."** Lise sees **Wenna's** ruined face in the courtyard. Sera breaks down in an alley and prays aloud. Providence doesn't come. Supervising nun **Maret** follows her ("Your face… Mine, thirty years ago"). Maret once thought of studying the rot itself to understand what they're fighting for, but was too afraid of the answer. She chose her "filling" (faith). When she arrived 40 years ago the border was three weeks west. She gives Sera a purse ("The habit is yours. You earned it") and glances toward the military compound: people there study the enemy. *(Note: the doc still has a leftover AI chat line in the middle, "Got it — back to Chapter 12…". See continuity flags.)*

### Ch 13: The Trader's Afternoon
Ryn sketches a casing in his field journal under a garden tree (the journal holds **five years** of notes, which is also the full extent of his memory) and works on the recoil problem. Aldric: "A hundred." / "Morning." On the street he says "Good afternoon, sister" to a nun who's clearly been crying (Sera). The tree fragment hits. He can't stop thinking about her. Sera goes to the compound gatehouse without knowing why. Vorne ("Can you speak? Are you lost? Are you hurt?") gets nothing. **Seris** takes the room: "Sit straight. Chin up. Fix your collar… Does Maret know you're gone? Of course she does." Sera: **"I want to know if my life mattered… I want to know what her will actually is."** Seris sends her to an inn: "Be at this gate early morning." Vorne: "Not like you, taking in a lost stray." / "This stray could be a lot of things. Lost isn't one of them." At the inn the lobby gossips about a nun needing a room. Ryn finds the angle to speak to her and chooses not to. A full meal appears outside her door with no name.

### Ch 14: The Road, The Site, The Camp
On the road Varen asks about the nuns. Mara: "Fools." He's startled that gods still exist (**Providence, "the Profaned Goddess"**). "Caen told me." / "Who is Caen." / "Caen is… Caen." He tells her the whole Yharim story. She's never heard any of it and thinks Caen was lying. **"I'm going to find him."** / "What are you going to say next — you'll find Providence too?" He's actually thinking about it: "Do you think the church would know where she is." The battle site has been swallowed by the rot. They make a hidden camp on a hill. Mara confesses: she stowed away on the seven's wagon to steal, then ran when they were attacked, and she tried to rob Varen because she thought he'd kill her. He doesn't judge (a rabbit lies to a wolf; survival is survival): "Are you hungry." He kills a camouflaged rabbit with a thrown rock from an impossible distance. A **Corrupter** (three times the size of a direwolf, eyeless, rows of claws, built to destroy) scouts the camp, then is "called" away. Mara wants out. Varen: "I must continue… I'll pay back what I owe you." / "Fine, but if things get rough, I'm leaving." He sets a perimeter, stacks wood for days, and plants torches where the trees narrow.

### Ch 15: What Comes Next
Ryn visits Aldric, who is building a **clock escapement** (mainspring, club-toothed escape wheel, pallet fork: tic, tic). "Time is our subject." They work for hours as if they've shared a workshop for decades, fixing tension and alloy, but wear at the contact points keeps causing drift. Ryn loses track of time for the first time in five years. Sera returns the platter. The innkeeper didn't send it. At the gate she copies the students' **"Magister."** Seris questions her: "What if you don't like the truth?" / "Once I find the answer, then I'll worry about whether I like it… I want to find Providence." / "And you're the one who's going to change it." / "And why not." **"I know the truth of the rot."** Deal: research assistant and records work in exchange for knowledge. **The Training Ground:** the five students (Bael, Iren, Davan, Solen, Myre) resent that she skipped the trials. Seris renames "divine energy" to **mana** and teaches the **cowl** (keeping mana in the whole body instead of pushing it through the palms). Bael jabs, "I thought you nuns loved dying out there," and Seris cuts him off: "That's enough." Sera's cowl is huge, bright, and burns without heat. Ryn introduces himself as "**Ryn Casvel**. Trader, inventor, entrepreneur." Seris: "And an alchemist." / "Just a humble pyrotechnician." He greets Sera ("A pleasure to see you again, sister"). She doesn't remember him, and the tree flash hits again. He smirks at Bael. Seris quietly arranges a dorm bed and a grey uniform. Sera lays her habit in the chest: kept, not discarded.

### Ch 16: The Cabin Between Worlds 2 *(in-doc heading wrongly says "Chapter Fourteen")*

### Ch 17: A Trader, A Wolf, And A Thief
Under the tree Ryn works out the equal-and-opposite law himself: recoil is a law, not a flaw. He needs a material that's soft at rest and hard under shock (non-Newtonian). Crystallized **Mureling ooze** does it backwards: it flows under slow pressure and shatters under shock. ### Ch 18: What the Blood Left Behind
**Lyris** is cultivating **Brask**, a village boy with raw shockwave magic ("Take more than feels safe… Direction comes after capacity"). Caen appears, visible only to her: "Impressive specimen." He makes her a double agent: report to the Weaver only what he allows, and he guarantees her survival when the Weaver discards her. "Yes, **Lord Signus**." Sera's routine settles in. She learns only five students were chosen out of many qualified applicants, and feels like a fraud. Myre: "She wouldn't have taken you in if she didn't see something worth taking." Vorne's men head out to test the finished rifles. Seris's office: a runic book, a script nobody speaks that **stores mana in objects**. Pylons (church-only kingdom-to-kingdom travel) need constant feeding, but runes supply themselves. Seris shows her an **ancient self-powered lantern** whose runes redraw its missing pieces. Her exact replica doesn't work. She calls them **enchanted artifacts**, and a few are locked away in the capital castle. **The truth of the rot:** the church's "Unmaking" doctrine is mostly invention. There's also "**crawling flesh**" in the **Scorched Expanse**. The gods were real, many of them, and they died in a war. The rot is a dead god's flesh and blood, and the **Crimson** is another. There's no evidence Providence was in the war; if she survived, she may be the **last living goddess**. Sera's purification was never divine: it's a branch of **healing magic** that predates the church.

### Ch 19: Into the Quiet
Varen's wounds heal unnaturally fast. He takes six Corrupter paws as proof and hangs them in a tree. Mara ("six gold is six hundred silver is sixty thousand copper," drooling) rejects Ryn's **writs** as "fancy paper", so he pays three gold up front. In the rot a **World Feeder** attacks. Varen saves Mara, the acid burns his calf, and his **knife melts**. They realise "It only finds us when we move." Ryn fires into the ground to draw it and Varen halves it. **The sword is untouched by the acid** even though Ryn says it's ordinary steel. Ryn gives him a **restoration potion** (20 silver). In the corrupted forest they fight many small rot creatures. Mara offers her poor little knife. Varen refuses until he sees Ryn nudging on her behalf and accepts it ("Thank you. It'll do"), reading her feelings for the first time. The **Murel village**: twenty glass enclosures, each with an acid-melted hole in the stone instead of the glass. Varen explains that grown Murelings merge on contact. They follow the damage to a looted granary (it's still an animal and needs to eat), then water, and Mara says: **the well.** It's sealed by a mineralized, glass-hard Blighted Murel crust. Ryn works all night with a mineral-then-organic acid process and cuts out a chunk. Mara, hurt: "I'm going to get stronger. I won't be in your way anymore." / Varen: "We'll return… you won't need to be in danger anymore." She hears the arrangement ending. Ryn asks what Varen wants. Caen, unseen, nudges the embers into the shape of the village and the verse. **"I want to find who burned the world… I will burn them."** Caen sees the blue life force rise and is satisfied.

### Ch 20: The City in the Morning

In the market, a child pickpocket and a drunk against a wall. Offense lesson ("the cowl is a funnel"): Bael's fire only scorches the wall, Myre's lightning spreads everywhere, Davan's air pressure snaps the post, Iren crushes the target with thorn vines, and Solen knocks the target off clean. "At least the enemies will be clean." Seris cuts a deep slash in the wall with a water blade, no cowl and no preparation. Vorne complains about the budget. Seris: "It was your men who destroyed the wall the first time." **Mana current:** everyone succeeds, and Myre makes **ball lightning** ("Well done"). ### Ch 21: What the Well Kept
Ryn's dream changes. The gold-haired woman's smile drops: **"What have you done to me."** Caen watches, sees Ryn's life force, and is impressed. World Feeders surround the camp. Varen and Ryn draw them away with thrown rocks and gunshots, and Ryn works out their attraction order (impact, then vibration, then warmth). Then the worms vanish because Caen has steered them into the well. The seal dissolves from below. The **Blighted Murel** (twenty Murelings fused into one mass taller than the houses, with a dark nerve cluster at the core) rises, samples the worms and spits them out, and hunts the crystal chunk Ryn took. Bullets are simply absorbed. Mara runs the rooftops ("You still owe me three gold"). Varen carries the crystal as bait. **The sword cuts it**, freezes it, pulses like its heartbeat, and pulls toward the dark nerve spots. Ryn improvises **incendiary rounds**. The creature smothers the fire with acid, then sheds its burning gel on purpose. **Mara's plan:** set the tightly packed houses on fire as a ring so every piece it sheds lands in more fire. Varen throws her the crystal ("Trust me"), cuts and gets the nerve cluster exposed, and the creature splits and re-fuses. He lights his gel-coated sword into a **flaming sword**, Ryn's last two rounds open a channel, and Varen drives it home. The bond fails for good and the pieces burn separately inside the ring.

## ARC 3: What The Tyrant Left Behind (the Godless Hand attack Ordenmere)

### Ch 22: Part 1
At curfew two "pilgrims from Varell" arrive at the western gate on foot, claiming bandits killed six of their companions, and swear by Providence. The guards (**Corren, Aldun**, Gate Watch Second Division) log them as "**Highmore and Emmeline**". Sera goes to Seris's office. Twenty years of books, fragments about lost languages and civilizations, and in a children's anthology (*A Thousand Nights and One — Tales Gathered from the Ends of the World*) a story of a boy pulled from hell flame by **"the dragon of rebirth"**: *"…and they called him tyrant, and he did not refuse the name… the boy walked back into the hell flame… And the dragon of rebirth laid itself down beside the fire and did not rise again."* The word "**dragon**" appears in ancient texts but its meaning has been erased, and there's a pre-Providence group whose writing reveres it. Seris believes someone spent **centuries systematically erasing** this history ("the kind of institution that outlives the people running it"). She has read the libraries of Ordenmere, Sundermere, Miredan and Ilyrath. Sera, who lost her mother at four and still remembers "The Prince and the Fox": **"No one can erase a fairy tale."** The stories are numbered, so the traveller's route could lead to where the tale came from. Seris has been in Ordenmere two years under a deal (unlimited capital library access in exchange for teaching), the library gave her nothing, and now leaving would mean leaving the students. Seris merges cowls with Sera and tries to copy her nature. It fails: Sera's mana is **"viscous."** "Could this be the blessing of Providence." / "You can call it what you want. For now." Alone with the lantern, Seris realises the runes pull ordinary mana, so whoever made it used **better quality** mana, not more. **Quality versus quantity.** In the dorm: Vorne goes red whenever Seris speaks to him ("that man is in love"). Vorne reads the gate log and works it out: not thieves, not Alister's people; the lie is the gate. Assassination, with a target beyond this district. He orders a quiet sweep, alerts the northern brigade (**Lieutenant Haes**) and the southern gate, and rides south, because Ordenmere is a **spiral** and the Capital can only be reached through every district. Up north, **Elysia and Severin** drive a carriage at Haes's squad after curfew, reciting the verse, and burn and blast the northern compound. "We are the heretics. The Godless Hand will burn what the tyrant left behind." Haes sends a runner to the chapel. **Harold** hands out Ryn-design crossbows and Severin takes a bolt to the shoulder. **Father Ignatius** (73, with cold blue eyes) rings the great chapel bell and sends **Tobias, Julian, Gideon and Leonard** north. Elysia fires a beacon into the sky. On the roof by the western gate the real Highmore and Emmeline move, and Highmore **cuts a rider and his horse in half with invisible air**: "We're not planning on staying long."

### Ch 23: Part 2

What type of plan was this." / "It's moronic." Seris appears: "The only one defenseless here is you." Highmore's invisible air meets her water barrier. "**Highmore of Oakhaven.** You nearly started a war twenty years ago." Seris uses water tentacles and a water whip, and breaks the gate with Highmore through it.
